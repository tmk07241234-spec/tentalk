# Reply to App Review: Guideline 1.2 (submission of November 2026)

Sent from App Store Connect → App Review, with build 1.0.1 (12).

Hello, and thank you for the review.

Tentalk is a language-learning app for speaking practice in one-on-one, 10-minute voice calls. It has no public feed, no posts, no text chat, no comments and no anonymous posting: the only user content is each person's profile (display name, short bio, optional photo and voice intro) and the live voice call itself, which is not recorded.

Build 1.0.1 (12) implements every precaution listed in your message:

1. Age rating 18+. The age rating is 18+, and sign-up asks for the date of birth and refuses anyone under 18.

2. Terms (EULA) with zero tolerance. On the first screen, people must tick "I agree to the Terms of Service and Community Guidelines" before they can sign up. The text above the box and the Terms (section 5) state that there is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. Links to the Terms, Community Guidelines and Privacy Policy are on the same screen and under Me → Terms and policies.

3. Filtering objectionable content. Display names and bios are checked against a word filter for objectionable words and contact details (phone numbers, emails, social media handles) before they can be saved.

4. Flagging objectionable content. During every call, the flag button reports the other person and ends the call in one tap. Every profile has a "Report" link as well.

5. Blocking abusive users. Every profile has a "Block" link. Reporting someone also blocks them automatically. Blocked people are never matched again (enforced on the server) and can no longer send requests.

6. Immediately removing content from the user's view. As soon as a person is reported or blocked, they disappear from the reporter's Find list, Home, Connections and History.

7. Acting on reports within 24 hours. Every new report sends a push notification to our moderators, and we review each report within 24 hours (stated in the Terms and in the app when a report is sent). When a violation is confirmed, the offending bio, photo and voice intro are removed from the profile, and the user who provided them is suspended. Repeat offenders are suspended again.

8. Contact information in the app. Me → Contact us lets people send a message to our team (including safety reports), and the support address tentalk.support@gmail.com is shown in the Terms, Privacy Policy and the App Store listing.

To see these on a device: sign out, then the first screen shows the agreement checkbox; with the demo account, open Find → any profile → "Report" / "Block" at the bottom; start a call from Home and tap the flag button.

Demo accounts are in App Review Information (account A in the sign-in fields, account B in the notes).

Thank you,
Tomoki Watanabe (Tentalk)
