Sent from App Store Connect → App Review, with build 1.0.1 (13).

Hello, and thank you for the detailed feedback.

Guideline 1.1: we removed the age wording from the description in every language. The store texts now describe only language practice and the safety features.

Guideline 1.2 (random and anonymous chat): quick matches no longer connect people directly. Build 1.0.1 (13) changes the flow:

1. Identifiable information before the call: when the app finds someone, it first shows a card with the person's display name, profile photo, native and learning languages, level, "language only" badge, bio and number of completed calls. No audio is connected at this point.
2. Accept, decline or skip: each person chooses "Talk" or "Skip". The call starts only when both choose "Talk" within 25 seconds. If either skips or does not answer, no call starts, and the two are not matched with each other again in that search. The server enforces this: the audio room cannot be joined until both have chosen "Talk".

Requested calls work the same way: the request shows who is calling, and the receiver accepts or declines.

All the earlier precautions remain: 18+ age rating and age check at sign-up, required agreement to the Terms and Community Guidelines (zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users), a filter for names and bios, report and block on every profile and during every call (a report also blocks), push alerts to our moderators with review within 24 hours, removal of offending content and suspension of the user, and Me > Contact us in the app.

To try it: sign in with demo account A on one device and B on another (or the web version at https://tentalk.expo.app), choose "Language exchange" on Home on both, and tap the call button. Each side sees the other person's card and chooses "Talk" or "Skip".
