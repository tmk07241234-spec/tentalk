# App Store listing (Tentalk 1.0)

Positioning: a **speaking-practice app** with structured 10-minute calls, not a chat or
social app. App Store guideline 1.2 says apps used mainly for "random or anonymous chat"
or "Chatroulette-style experiences" do not belong on the store, so every text leads with
learning, structure and safety, and avoids words like "chat", "meet", "friends",
"strangers" and "random chat". The random call is described as "quick match with a
practice partner".

Limits: name 30, subtitle 30, promotional text 170, keywords 100 (comma-separated, no
spaces), description 4000.

Tentalk は「話す力」を伸ばすための語学アプリです。学んでいる言語で、1対1の音声通話を10分ずつ重ねていきます。

・1回の通話は10分が基本。終わりが決まっているから、気軽に始められます

- Name: Tentalk
- Subtitle: Speak a language in 10 minutes
- Promotional text: Practise speaking English, Japanese, Korean or Spanish in 1-on-1 voice calls of 10 minutes. Question cards and help phrases keep the talk going, even for beginners.
- Keywords: speaking,language exchange,pronunciation,english,japanese,korean,spanish,conversation,practice
- Description:

Tentalk is a language-learning app for one thing: speaking. You practise in one-on-one voice calls of 10 minutes each, in the language you are learning.

10 FOCUSED MINUTES
- Every call has a clear length, so it is easy to start
- Finish 10 minutes to grow your streak and earn badges

TWO PRACTICE MODES
- Practice buddies: people who learn the same language, with different native languages, speak only that language
- Language exchange: practise with a native speaker and teach each other your languages

CONVERSATION THAT KEEPS GOING
- A question card every call. You both talk about the same question, and either of you can move on to the next one
- Read each question in your own language too
- Help phrases for when you get stuck, and a gentle prompt when it goes quiet

BUILT FOR SAFE PRACTICE
- 18+ only
- A "language only" badge for people who are here just to learn
- Report and block at any time, during calls and from profiles
- Our team reviews reports and suspends accounts that break the guidelines
- No messaging and no timeline. You can follow only people you finished a 10-minute call with

LANGUAGES
English, Japanese, Korean and Spanish (the app itself is in all four)
